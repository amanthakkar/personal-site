# Content Improvement Questions

Answer these honestly - they'll help us craft a more authentic, memorable website.

## The Big Picture Questions

1. **What's the one thing you want visitors to remember about you?** (Not your job title or skills - something deeper)

2. **What problem keeps you up at night?** (Technical, personal, societal - anything you genuinely care about)

3. **What's a moment in your career that changed how you think?** (A failure, a breakthrough, a conversation)

4. **Why did you REALLY build this website?** (Beyond "to avoid running a script" - what was the deeper motivation?)

5. **What do you believe about technology/engineering that others might disagree with?** (Your contrarian take)

## Personal Story Questions

6. **What's the story behind your interest in finance/crypto?** (Not just "I watched MIT lectures" - what clicked? What fascinated you?)

7. **Why Chelsea FC?** (What's the story? When did you become a fan? What does it mean to you?)

8. **What's something you've learned about yourself through your work?** (Not a skill - a personal insight)

9. **What's a project you're most proud of and WHY?** (Not the most impressive technically - the one that meant something to you)

10. **What's something you've failed at that taught you something valuable?** (Be specific)

## The "Why This Website" Section - Deeper Questions

11. **What was the moment you decided "I need to build this myself"?** (What was the frustration? The "aha" moment?)

12. **What did you learn about yourself while building it?** (Not technical skills - personal insights)

13. **What surprised you most about the process?** (What didn't go as expected?)

14. **If someone asked "why should I care about your website?", what would you say?** (Be honest - what's the value?)

## Voice & Tone Questions

15. **How do you want people to feel when they read your site?** (Inspired? Curious? Comfortable reaching out?)

16. **What's your communication style?** (Direct? Storytelling? Technical but accessible? Humorous?)

17. **Who's your ideal reader?** (Recruiters? Fellow engineers? People interested in finance? Yourself in 5 years?)

## The Crypto/Finance Section - Make It Current

18. **Why did you step back from crypto/finance content?** (Be honest - was it the bear market? Lost interest? Too time-consuming?)

19. **What's your current relationship with that interest?** (Still learning? Observing? Waiting for the right moment?)

20. **What would make you want to write about it again?** (What would re-ignite that passion?)

## The Meta Question

21. **If you had to describe yourself in 3 words (not job titles), what would they be?**

22. **What's something you're working on becoming better at?** (Personally or professionally)

23. **What's a question you wish more people would ask you?**

---

## Quick Wins - Answer These for Immediate Improvements:

**For the About section:**
- What's one sentence that captures who you are beyond your job?
- What's a hobby or interest that reveals something about your personality?
- What's something you're curious about right now?

**For the "Why This Website" section:**
- What was the actual frustration that led to building this? (Be specific)
- What's the one thing you're most proud of about how it turned out?
- What would you tell someone who's thinking about building their own site?

**For Projects:**
- Which project taught you the most? (Not technically - personally/professionally)
- What's a project that didn't work out but was valuable anyway?
- What's something you'd build if you had unlimited time?

---

Answer as many as you want - even partial answers help! The goal is to find your authentic voice, not create a "perfect" persona.
