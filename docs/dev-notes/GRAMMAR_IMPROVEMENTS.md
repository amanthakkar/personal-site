## About Section (src/data/about.md)

- Current: "If you're a recruiter, please check out my [resume](resume) and [projects](projects) and reach out to me @ [amanbthakkar@gmail.com](mailto:amanbthakkar@gmail.com) or DM me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/amanbthakkar/)! As of now I am not looking for new opportunities."

- Changes: "reach out to me @" → "reach out via" (more professional), "DM me" → removed (redundant), "As of now I am" → "I'm currently" (more concise)

**Line 7:**
- Current: "During COVID when I was "Working From Home", I got extremely interested in finance..."
- Improved: "During COVID, while working from home, I became extremely interested in finance..."
- Changes: Removed quotes around "Working From Home" (not needed), "when I was" → "while" (more natural), "got" → "became" (more professional)

**Line 7 (continued):**
- Current: "...where I learnt more about derivatives and their pricing."
- Improved: "...where I learned more about derivatives and their pricing."
- Changes: "learnt" → "learned" (American English, more common in tech)

**Line 9:**
- Current: "Though I haven't been actively talking about finance and crypto for the last 2-3 years (co-inciding with what can be called as the crypto bear market), my interest in this field is alive."

- Changes: "Though" → "Although" (more formal), "talking about" → "discussing" (more professional), "co-inciding" → "coinciding" (correct spelling), "is alive" → "remains strong" (more professional)

**Line 13:**

**Line 17:**
